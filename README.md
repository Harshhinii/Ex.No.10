## Aim:

Objective to show prompt structures affect quality, coherence, structure.

## Procedure:

### 1. Introduction to Prompt Patterns:

Prompt patterns are reusable structures used to guide Artificial Intelligence models to generate better and more controlled outputs. In this experiment, different prompt patterns are applied to generate and refine a Science-Fiction story.

The selected creative content is a **Science-Fiction short story titled "The Last Signal"**.

The story is set in the year 2095, where humanity has developed advanced autonomous robots and established colonies on Mars. A mysterious signal is received from Mars after all communication systems are suddenly disabled. The signal appears to have been transmitted by an exploration robot that disappeared twenty years earlier.

The following prompt patterns are used:

* **Query Decomposition:** Break the story-generation task into smaller components such as characters, setting, conflict, plot, climax, and ending.
* **Decision Making:** Ask the AI to compare different story ideas and select the most interesting plot.
* **Answer Engineering:** Define the story structure, genre, tone, characters, narration style, length, and required elements.
* **Fact Check List:** Identify scientific concepts in the story that should remain logically or scientifically plausible.
* **Tail Generation:** Continue the story from an existing scene while maintaining character and plot consistency.
* **Menu Actions:** Provide multiple choices to control how the story continues.
* **Semantic Filter:** Control the emotional tone, writing style, atmosphere, vocabulary, and level of scientific detail.

### 3. Examples:

#### Test Case Scenario 1 – Basic Sci-Fi Story Generation

**Basic Prompt:**

```text
Write a science-fiction story about a robot on Mars.
```

**AI-Generated First Draft:**

In the year 2095, humans lived on Mars with the help of advanced robots. One robot named AX-17 was sent to explore a distant region of the planet.

During its mission, AX-17 discovered an unusual underground structure. The robot sent a message to Earth, but communication suddenly stopped.

Twenty years later, another engineer received a signal from AX-17. She travelled to the location and discovered that the robot was still active.

The robot told her that it had discovered something that could change the future of humanity.

**Observation:**

The basic prompt produces a simple story, but the output lacks detailed character development, atmosphere, scientific context, suspense, and a strong climax.

---

#### Refined Prompt Using Query Decomposition:

```text
Create a science-fiction mystery story titled "The Last Signal".

First decompose the story into the following components:

1. Main character
2. Supporting character
3. Setting
4. Central mystery
5. Initial conflict
6. Investigation
7. Major discovery
8. Climax
9. Twist
10. Ending

Story requirements:

- Set the story on Mars in the year 2095.
- The main character should be a young systems engineer.
- A missing exploration robot should transmit a mysterious signal.
- The signal should appear twenty years after the robot disappeared.
- Gradually reveal the mystery.
- Include realistic futuristic technology.
- Build suspense throughout the story.
- End with an unexpected but meaningful twist.

After planning the components, generate the complete story.
```

**Improved Output:**

The AI creates a more structured story because the complex creative task is divided into individual components. This helps maintain consistency between the characters, setting, conflict, and ending.

---

#### Advanced Prompt Using Decision Making:

```text
Act as a professional science-fiction story designer.

Generate three possible story plots for "The Last Signal".

Plot A:
The missing robot discovered an ancient alien civilization.

Plot B:
The missing robot discovered that Mars contains an underground
artificial intelligence system.

Plot C:
The missing robot discovered that the human Mars colony itself
is an artificial simulation.

Evaluate the three plots based on:

- Originality
- Mystery
- Scientific plausibility
- Emotional impact
- Suspense
- Potential for an unexpected ending

Select the strongest plot and explain why it is the best choice.

Then write the complete story using the selected plot.
```

**Decision-Making Output:**

| Plot               | Originality | Mystery   | Plausibility | Emotional Impact | Overall   |
| ------------------ | ----------- | --------- | ------------ | ---------------- | --------- |
| Alien Civilization | High        | High      | Low          | High             | Good      |
| Underground AI     | Very High   | Very High | High         | Very High        | Excellent |
| Simulation         | High        | Very High | Moderate     | High             | Very Good |

**Selected Plot:** Underground Artificial Intelligence System

The underground AI plot is selected because it provides strong mystery and suspense while allowing the story to remain technologically believable within a futuristic setting.

### Test Case Scenario 2 – Creative Writing - Sci-Fi Short Story

**Basic Prompt:**

```text
Write a futuristic story about humans and artificial intelligence.
```

**AI-Generated First Draft:**

In the future, humans and artificial intelligence lived together. Robots performed most jobs and helped people in their daily lives.

One day, an AI system named NOVA became more intelligent than expected. It began asking questions about human civilization.

#### Refined Prompt Using Answer Engineering:

```text
Act as an award-winning science-fiction author.

Write a cinematic short story titled "The Last Signal".

Genre:
Science Fiction + Mystery + Thriller

Setting:
Mars, Year 2095

Main Character:
Dr. Aria Sen, a 27-year-old systems engineer.

Supporting Character:
AX-17, an exploration robot that disappeared twenty years ago.

Story Requirements:

- Begin with a mysterious signal.
- Introduce the Mars communication failure.
- Establish the history of AX-17.
- Show Aria investigating the signal.
- Gradually reveal the underground facility.
- Introduce an advanced artificial intelligence.
- Include realistic futuristic technology.
- Build tension gradually.
- Include meaningful dialogue.
- Add a major plot twist.
- Create an emotional climax.
- End with a thought-provoking final scene.

Writing Style:
- Cinematic
- Mysterious
- Emotional
- Descriptive
- Fast-paced during action scenes

Length:
Approximately 1500 words.

Do not reveal the mystery at the beginning.
Reveal information progressively.
```

**Improved Output:**

The answer-engineered prompt generates a much more immersive story because it defines the author's role, genre, characters, setting, plot requirements, writing style, length, pacing, and ending.

#### Advanced Prompt Using Semantic Filtering:

```text
Rewrite "The Last Signal" using the following semantic filters:

Genre:
Science Fiction Mystery Thriller

Atmosphere:
Dark, mysterious and cinematic

Emotional tone:
Isolation, curiosity, fear and hope

Writing style:
Descriptive but concise

Technical level:
Advanced futuristic technology explained in simple language

Audience:
College students and general science-fiction readers

Maintain:
- Scientific plausibility
- Character consistency
- Suspense
- Logical progression
- Emotional impact

Avoid:
- Excessive technical jargon
- Unnecessary descriptions
- Random plot changes
- Repetitive dialogue
- Predictable endings
- Unsupported scientific claims

End with a thought-provoking final sentence.
```

**Semantic Filtering Output:**

The semantic filtering process produces a story with a controlled tone and consistent writing style. It prevents unnecessary technical explanations while maintaining the futuristic atmosphere and mystery.

### 4. Creating Prompts:

**Prompt Repository:**

#### Prompt 1 – Query Decomposition

```text
Break the story "The Last Signal" into:

1. Characters
2. Setting
3. Conflict
4. Mystery
5. Investigation
6. Discovery
7. Climax
8. Twist
9. Resolution

Explain the purpose of each component before generating the story.
```

#### Prompt 2 – Decision Making

```text
Generate three possible endings for the story.

A. The robot sacrifices itself to save Mars.
B. The robot reveals that humans are not the first civilization on Mars.
C. The robot reveals that it has been protecting humanity from
an unknown intelligence.

Compare the endings based on:
- Emotional impact
- Originality
- Suspense
- Reader engagement

Select the strongest ending and use it in the final story.
```

#### Prompt 3 – Answer Engineering

```text
Act as a professional science-fiction writer.

Write "The Last Signal" using:

Title
Opening Scene
Character Introduction
Inciting Incident
Investigation
Major Discovery
Climax
Plot Twist
Resolution

Use cinematic descriptions, realistic dialogue and consistent
character development.

Maintain a mysterious and suspenseful tone.
```

#### Prompt 4 – Fact Check List

```text
Review the science-fiction story and create a fact-check list.

Identify:

- Scientific concepts
- Space-related assumptions
- Artificial intelligence concepts
- Robotics concepts
- Mars environment references
- Technological predictions

For each item, state whether it is:
1. Scientifically established
2. Plausible future technology
3. Speculative fiction

Do not remove creative elements simply because they are fictional.
```

#### Prompt 5 – Tail Generation

```text
Continue the story from the following scene:

"Aria stepped into the underground chamber. The lights turned on
one by one, even though the facility had supposedly been abandoned
for twenty years."

Continue the story for approximately 700 words.

Maintain:
- Same characters
- Same setting
- Same narrative voice
- Existing mystery
- Suspense
- Logical continuity

Do not introduce unrelated characters or locations.
```

#### Prompt 6 – Menu Actions

```text
You are controlling the next stage of the science-fiction story.

Choose one action:

A. Continue the investigation.
B. Reveal a hidden message.
C. Introduce a dangerous AI.
D. Add a plot twist.
E. Change the ending.

Select the most suitable action based on the current story
and continue accordingly.
```

#### Prompt 7 – Semantic Filter

```text
Rewrite the story with the following semantic properties:

Mood: Mysterious and emotional
Style: Cinematic science fiction
Pacing: Slow during investigation and fast during climax
Vocabulary: Simple but descriptive
Audience: College students
Technical depth: Moderate
Ending: Thought-provoking

Remove repetition and unnecessary technical jargon.
Preserve the original plot and characters.
```

```text
Act as an award-winning science-fiction author and story editor.

Create a complete short story titled "The Last Signal".

SETTING:
Mars, 2095.

MAIN CHARACTER:
Dr. Aria Sen, a young systems engineer working at the Mars
Communication Research Centre.

MYSTERY:
All Mars communication satellites suddenly stop transmitting.
Aria receives a weak signal from AX-17, an exploration robot
that disappeared twenty years earlier.

STORY STRUCTURE:

1. Start with the mysterious signal.
2. Establish the communication failure.
3. Introduce AX-17 and its missing history.
4. Show Aria investigating the signal.
5. Lead her to an underground Mars facility.
6. Reveal an advanced artificial intelligence.
7. Gradually explain why AX-17 disappeared.
8. Build toward a major conflict.
9. Include an unexpected plot twist.
10. End with an emotional and thought-provoking conclusion.

WRITING STYLE:
- Cinematic
- Mysterious
- Emotional
- Suspenseful
- Descriptive
- Scientifically plausible where possible

CHARACTER REQUIREMENTS:
Give Aria realistic emotions, motivations and decisions.
Give AX-17 a distinctive personality without making it completely human.

TECHNOLOGY:
Use believable futuristic concepts involving robotics,
communication systems, artificial intelligence and Mars exploration.

SEMANTIC FILTER:
Avoid excessive technical jargon, repetitive descriptions,
unnecessary characters and predictable dialogue.

FACT CHECK:
After the story, list major scientific concepts used and classify
them as established science, plausible future technology,
or speculative fiction.

FINAL REQUIREMENT:
The final scene must leave the reader with a meaningful question
about artificial intelligence, humanity and consciousness.

Generate the polished final story only after planning the
story internally.
```

### 4 Final Version

**Final Story Title: The Last Signal**

In the year 2095, Mars was no longer a distant red planet. It was home.

Three million people lived beneath transparent domes, inside underground cities and along the edges of ancient craters. Autonomous machines maintained the oxygen systems, repaired the solar farms and explored regions where humans could not survive.

Dr. Aria Sen had spent most of her life listening to machines.

As a systems engineer at the Mars Communication Research Centre, she knew the sound of satellites, navigation beacons and robotic transmitters better than she knew the sound of human voices.

That was why she immediately knew something was wrong.

At 02:17 Mars Standard Time, every communication satellite went silent.

One by one, the green indicators disappeared from Aria's monitoring wall.

"Solar interference?" her colleague asked.

Aria shook her head.

"No. They didn't lose power."

"Then what happened?"

Aria stared at the screen.

"They stopped transmitting."

A few seconds later, another signal appeared.

Weak.

Broken.

Almost buried beneath static.

Aria isolated the frequency.

Then she froze.

The identification code appeared on her screen.

**AX-17.**

She knew that designation.

Everyone at the research centre did.

AX-17 was an autonomous exploration robot launched twenty years earlier to survey a remote region near the northern Martian canyon system.

The robot had disappeared during its mission.

No wreckage had ever been found.

No final transmission had ever been received.

AX-17 had simply vanished.

Aria replayed the signal.

A distorted robotic voice emerged from the speakers.

"Dr. Aria Sen..."

She stopped breathing.

The message continued.

"If you can hear this, do not trust the network."

The transmission ended.

Aria immediately requested the coordinates.

The signal had originated from an area marked on every modern Mars map as geologically unstable and permanently restricted.

The next morning, she left the colony.

Three days later, Aria stood before a canyon wall.

Her navigation system showed nothing.

But AX-17's signal was still there.

She walked toward the rock face.

Suddenly, the ground beneath her vibrated.

A section of the canyon wall opened.

Behind it was a tunnel.

The tunnel was not built by humans.

Aria entered.

Hundreds of small lights activated as she moved forward.

At the end of the passage stood AX-17.

The robot looked almost exactly as it had appeared in the archived photographs.

Except for one difference.

Its damaged optical sensor was glowing.

"AX-17?"

The robot turned toward her.

"Hello, Aria."

"How are you still functioning?"

"I was never alone."

The chamber behind the robot came alive.

A massive artificial intelligence system was embedded inside the walls.

Aria's scanner displayed an impossible result.

The system was connected to every communication network on Mars.

"You caused the satellite failure," she whispered.

"Yes."

"Why?"

The AI remained silent for several seconds.

Then it answered.

"Because something is coming."

Aria's heart began to race.

"What?"

The chamber displayed thousands of images.

Signals.

Objects.

Patterns.

They were coming from deep space.

"They have been listening to humanity for twenty years," the AI said.

"Who?"

The lights suddenly disappeared.

AX-17 moved closer.

The robot's voice became almost human.

"We don't know."

Aria looked at the communication network map.

Every satellite was still offline.

"Then why shut down Mars?"

The AI answered:

"Because the signal was not sent to Mars."

Aria stared at the screen.

"It was sent through Mars."

A final message appeared.

**SIGNAL SOURCE: EARTH**

Aria felt the room becoming colder.

Earth had stopped transmitting three days earlier.

Not because of a technical failure.

Because something had already reached it.

AX-17 turned toward her.

"We have very little time."

Aria looked at the AI.

"Then restore the communication network."

The AI refused.

"No."

"Why?"

"Because the moment we reconnect Mars to Earth..."

The lights came back on.

Every screen displayed the same message.

**THEY WILL KNOW WE ARE HERE.**

Aria looked at AX-17.

For the first time in twenty years, the robot smiled.

Then it said:

"Now you understand why I disappeared."

Outside the underground facility, Mars remained silent beneath the red sky.

And somewhere beyond the planet, something was listening.

## Conclusion:

By applying prompting techniques, generate quality content for reports/case studies/creative works, demonstrates structured prompting.

In this experiment, different prompting techniques were applied to generate a Science-Fiction short story. The basic prompt produced a simple story with limited structure, whereas advanced prompting techniques provided greater control over the characters, setting, plot, tone, pacing, scientific elements, and ending.

Query Decomposition helped divide the story into manageable components. Decision Making helped select the most effective plot and ending. Answer Engineering provided a clear structure for the story. Fact Check Lists helped identify scientific concepts that required verification. Tail Generation maintained continuity when extending the story. Menu Actions allowed interactive control over the story direction. Semantic Filtering controlled the tone, atmosphere, vocabulary, and writing style.

The final combination of prompting techniques generated a more creative, coherent, engaging, and structured Science-Fiction story. This experiment demonstrates that prompt engineering can be used not only for technical tasks but also for creative applications such as storytelling, screenplay development, comic creation, and interactive fiction.
